# Workflows

The built-in workflows are all DAGs with specify, plan, implementation, test,
parallel reviews, consolidation, fix, final verification, build, and learning
stages. Names cover idea-to-product, zero-to-MVP, feature development, bug
fix, incident, product improvement, growth experiment, launch, pivot,
technical debt, customer request, and weekly founder review.

Shared stages keep the execution contract consistent. Domain-specific prefixes
change the methodology prompt. Provider integrations and specialized metric
fixtures are intentionally not implied by the workflow name.
