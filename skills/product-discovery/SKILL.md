# Product discovery method

1. Capture the observation and the user's exact statement separately.
2. State the underlying problem or job without prematurely naming a feature.
3. List assumptions and mark each as known, unknown, or contradicted.
4. Form falsifiable hypotheses with target user, behavior, mechanism, and
   expected outcome.
5. Gather the cheapest decision-relevant evidence. Label evidence separately
   from inference.
6. Choose a decision, record alternatives rejected and confidence, and define
   non-goals.
7. Translate the decision into testable acceptance criteria and a measurement
   plan. Do not call a brief or PRD implementation evidence.
