# Compound learning method

1. Summarize the objective and the verified result, not the intended result.
2. Record decisions, alternatives rejected, failures, fixes, experiments,
   patterns, and unresolved uncertainty.
3. Explain why the result happened and identify conditions under which the
   lesson may not generalize.
4. Add a retrieval title, tags, and a concrete future problem cue.
5. Remove secrets, personal data, and unnecessary raw logs before persistence.
6. In the next related cycle, retrieve the lesson and show where it changed a
   hypothesis, plan, implementation, test, or decision.
